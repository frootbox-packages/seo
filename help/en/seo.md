**SEO** shows what search engines see of each page: its title in the
search results and its description. Pages with hints come first.

## Title and description

The title in search results is the title of the page and the name of
the website. From about 60 characters, search engines cut it.

The description is the short text below it. Write it in the settings
of the page, under **Description for search engines**. 50 to 160
characters fit best. Without one, search engines show some text of the
page. Every page should have its own title and description.

Like every change to a page, a new description goes online when you
publish the page.

## Hints

- **Warning:** worth fixing, e.g. a missing description or the same
  title as another page.
- **Notice:** good to know, e.g. a description that is a bit long, or
  a page that is excluded from search engines.

## What happens by itself

The website lists every published page for search engines at
`/sitemap.xml`, in every language, and points to it in `/robots.txt`. Locked pages and pages excluded from search engines are
left out.

When someone shares a page, networks show its title, its description
and the first image on the page; for a news article its image.
